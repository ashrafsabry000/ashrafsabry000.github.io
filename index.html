Track Me - Campaign Performance & Reporting

Track Me is an internal automation and analytics utility designed to streamline advertising reporting, campaign performance monitoring, and lead data synchronization using workflow automation platforms.

Core Functionality

  • Automated Performance Reporting: Retrieves key advertising metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions, and cost) via the Google Ads API for internal business analysis.
  • Workflow Integration: Connects authorized advertising accounts with internal reporting dashboards and automation tools to eliminate manual data export.
  • AI-Assisted Insights: Summarizes historical performance metrics to assist internal marketing teams in optimizing budget allocation and ad spend efficiency.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Track Me ("we", "our", or "the Application") accesses, collects, uses, and safeguards data when interacting with Google APIs, specifically the Google Ads API.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When authorized by the account holder via Google OAuth 2.0, Track Me accesses the following information:

  • Google Ads Account Data: Campaign metrics, ad group performance, keyword statistics, cost metrics, and lead form submissions from authorized Google Ads accounts.
  • Basic Profile Information: Email address associated with the Google account for authentication and access verification purposes.

2. How We Use the Information

All data retrieved through the Google Ads API is used strictly for internal business operations, including:

  • Generating internal performance reports and analytics summaries.
  • Auditing campaign efficiency and conversion tracking.
  • Automating internal reporting workflows via secure middleware integrations.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Track Me's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell, rent, or lease Google user data or Google Ads data to any third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving third-party advertisements.
  • Data is only transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections to authorized internal workflow processors requested by the account owner.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We implement industry-standard security protocols, including OAuth 2.0 token encryption and secure HTTPS transmission. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely within the user's private automation environment. Users may revoke access at any time via their Google Account Security Settings. Upon revocation or request, all associated tokens and cached metrics are permanently deleted.
